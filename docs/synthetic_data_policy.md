# Synthetic Data Policy

1. Generate records without using real patient source data.
2. Use fictional names and clearly synthetic identifiers.
3. Do not include real medical records, contact details, or identifiers.
4. Do not accept real patient uploads.
5. Label demonstration datasets and reports as synthetic.
6. Keep credentials out of source code, notebooks, and reports.
7. Do not describe the application as HIPAA compliant solely because
   its demonstration dataset is synthetic.
8. Clinical values are fabricated examples, not medical advice.
